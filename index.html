ABOUT

I am an Electrical Engineer and LPDP Awardee, currently pursuing my Master's degree in Electrical Engineering at Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) . I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from Universitas Jenderal Soedirman, where I graduated as Valedictorian. When I was an undergraduate, I did a reseach internship at the NAIST (Nara Institute of Science and Technology) in Japan. Currently, I am looking for PhD opportunities in the field of robotics.

My work focuses on software robotic control using ROS 2, Gazebo, and Reinforcement Learning, alongside deep learning applications for medical image analysis. I am driven to develop solutions across both autonomous mobile systems and healthcare diagnostics.
